How a logo project works

01 Brief
You tell me about the business, who it is for and where the logo will live. The contact form or a short call is enough.
02 Quote
You get a fixed quote and a timeline in writing before any work starts, in NPR or AUD.
03 Design
I design the logo myself and you review it with me over video calls and shared files.
04 Delivery
You get your logo in every format you need, with guidelines that explain how to use it.
